CPU: what happens when a program runs?

The CPU fetches, decodes and executes instructions. Its control unit coordinates operations; the ALU performs arithmetic and logic; registers hold small amounts of data or control information temporarily.

In the CCEA factfile, the fetch–execute cycle is taught using the program counter, MAR, MDR, IR and ALU. Learn the roles of each, as well as the overall process.
